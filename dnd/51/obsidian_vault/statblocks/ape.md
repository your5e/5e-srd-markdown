_Medium beast, unaligned_

- **Armor Class** 12
- **Hit Points** 19 (3d8 + 6)
- **Speed** 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT    | WIS     | CHA    |
|---------|---------|---------|--------|---------|--------|
| 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 6 (-2) | 12 (+1) | 7 (-2) |

- **Skills** Athletics +5, Perception +3
- **Senses** passive Perception 13
- **Languages** —
